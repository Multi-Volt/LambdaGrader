How it works

  1. Students — import or type your roster (up to 400).
  2. Answer key — type the key as plain text, e.g. 1. B.
  3. Bubble sheets — print blank sheets, or personalised sheets with each student's name and ID pre-filled.
  4. Scan & grade — scan the completed sheets to a PDF (any resolution ≥ 150 dpi) and drop it in.
  5. Results — review flagged sheets, then download the CSV.

🔒 Everything — roster, keys, scans, scores — stays on this computer, in this browser. No data is uploaded anywhere. Use Backup to move your data to another browser.

Answer key

Key format 
1. A          one correct answer
2) c          case and punctuation don't matter
3 B 2         worth 2 points (default 1)
4 A,C         either A or C is accepted
5 *           free — everyone gets credit
6 -           dropped — not scored
# comments start with a hash

ABCDA DCBAB   or just letters in order;
[AC] B *      [..] groups, * and - work here too

Printing tips

  • Print at 100% or "fit to page" — both work; the corner squares handle scaling.
  • Don't change the exam settings after printing. The sheet records its own layout in the small squares along the top edge, so mismatches are detected.
  • Single-sided only. Scan in grayscale or colour; 200–300 dpi is ideal.
